In one breath

Super app = bundling many services into one app. Convenient, but regulated separately because of monopoly, forced bundling, and data concentration concerns.

A super app is a platform that bundles services like payments, messaging, shopping, and ride-hailing into a single app. It's highly convenient, but market power concentrates in one app, data can be combined and misused, and bundling can block competing services from getting in. That's why platform regulations such as online-platform fairness acts and the EU's Digital Markets Act (DMA) separately require interoperability and fair terms of entry.

When grown-ups say it

Super App
Many services bundled into one app. Comes with both convenience and concentration concerns.
Platform Regulation
A law that treats one dominant app differently. Includes things like online-platform fairness acts and the DMA.
Bundling/Tying
Blocking or forcing how other services get bundled in. A classic way competition gets restricted.
Data Concentration
Data all pooling into one app. Misuse here can cause outsized harm.
Interoperability
Working smoothly together with other services. One of the core conditions platform regulation demands.
Market Dominance
One service holding a tight grip on the market. Grow too large, and fair competition gets harder.
Digital Markets Act (DMA)
The EU's fair-competition law for platforms. Imposes interoperability duties on large platforms.
Network Neutrality
The principle banning discrimination on the network too. Shares a similar fairness tension. → everyone waits in the same lunch line
