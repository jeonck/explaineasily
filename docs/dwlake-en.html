What are a Data Warehouse and a Data Lake?

A data warehouse and a data lake, told as a story comparing a tidily shelved warehouse with a yard where things just get piled up.

Neat boxes and messy piles arrive all at once.Decide: shelve it now, or just pile it up for later?

Shelving it requires every sorting rule to be set in advance.A brand-new kind of item has no rule, so it can't go on the shelf.

A warehouse sorts things onto shelves; a lake piles them up exactly as they arrived.Depending on the goal, you often need both.

Warehouse: sort, then storethat's schema-on-write
Lake: store, then sortthat's schema-on-read
Warehouse: fast routine reportsaggregated straight away with SQL
Lake: keeps all kinds of raw inputphotos and logs kept as-is

The key difference is when you sort it.Warehouse sorts on write; lake sorts on read.

An unmanaged lake turns into a 'data swamp'.Analysis usually runs on the tidy warehouse side.

In one breath

A data warehouse is a tidy, pre-sorted warehouse; a data lake is a raw storage yard that keeps things exactly as they arrived.

A data warehouse fixes its schema before storing (schema-on-write), holding structured data well-suited to fast aggregation and reporting. A data lake stores first and interprets structure only when needed (schema-on-read), so it can keep anything — structured or not — exactly as it arrived. Left unmanaged, a lake easily becomes a 'data swamp'; a lakehouse approach tries to combine both.

When grown-ups say it

Schema-on-write
Sorting before anything goes in. The approach a warehouse uses.
Schema-on-read
Storing first, sorting when it's used. The approach a lake uses.
Structured/Unstructured Data
Neat boxes vs. messy piles. A lake takes in both kinds.
ETL/ELT
A difference in when the tidying happens. ETL tidies before the move, ELT after.
Data Swamp
A yard nobody's kept up. Becomes a pile nobody can search.
BI Reporting
The routine aggregate report. What a warehouse is best suited for.
Lakehouse
A yard with a few shelves added in. An approach combining warehouse and lake.
OLAP
Slicing the whole thing for analysis. Usually run on warehouse data. → the manager who slices the whole warehouse
