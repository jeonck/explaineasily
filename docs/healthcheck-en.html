In one breath

A health check = asking every ride 'are you okay?' every few seconds, and pulling it from the list when there's no answer — then letting it back in once it's well again.

A method of pinging a service at regular intervals to see if it's still alive. Whether it's alive (liveness) and whether it's ready for traffic (readiness) are two different questions, and the result feeds straight into a load balancer, updating the list of who's in service.

When grown-ups say it

Health check
Asking 'are you okay?' every few seconds. No answer means it's pulled from the list.
Liveness
The question 'are you alive at all?' Passing this alone doesn't mean everything's fine.
Readiness
The question 'are you ready for guests?' A ride can be alive but still not ready yet.
Ping / Heartbeat
The short click-signal sent out. The reply to this signal is what tells you the status.
Automatic removal and return
Dropping out on no answer, and returning once well again. No one has to manage this by hand.
Load balancer integration
Where the health-check result goes next. It only sends guests to rides that passed. → the usher who spreads guests across booths
Failover
Switching to another ride instead of one that's not answering. A failover can only start once the health check has already noticed.
Service discovery
The sign that shows which rides are currently open. Health-check results are what keep this sign always up to date.
