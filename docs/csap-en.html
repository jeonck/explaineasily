What is CSAP?

CSAP (Cloud Security Assurance Program), told as a story about the king's inspector checking a rented warehouse and stamping it.

The office wants to rent an outside warehouse, but isn't sure it's safe.The clerk isn't sure whether it's safe to trust the office's records to a rented warehouse (the cloud).

Every warehouse just claims to be safe.Everyone says they're safe, but with no common standard, there's no way to know who to believe.

CSAP is the king's inspector checking it and stamping it.It checks against a fixed list and stamps only the ones that pass. → ongoing door-checks on the rented warehouse continue even after the stamp.

Checked against a fixed listphysical security, access control, isolation

Re-inspected periodicallythe stamp doesn't last forever

The tier decides what kind of record can go there.The more sensitive the record, the higher the tier it needs.

The stamp alone doesn't end the story.It's a Korea-specific system that doesn't carry abroad, and the door-checking continues even after the stamp.

In one breath

CSAP = the king's inspector checks a rented warehouse (the cloud) against a fixed list and gives it a stamp. No stamp, and a government office can't rent it.

CSAP, Cloud Security Assurance Program. KISA (Korea Internet & Security Agency) reviews and certifies a cloud service's physical, technical, and managerial security level. Korean public agencies must use a CSAP-certified cloud service, and the certification is tiered (originally high/medium/low, now a more granular multi-tier system) by how sensitive the information is.

When grown-ups say it

CSAP
The king's safety stamp on a rented warehouse. Korea's Cloud Security Assurance Program.
KISA
The king's inspector who checks it. The Korea Internet & Security Agency, which runs the actual review.
Multi-tier system
Grading warehouses by how safe they are. Which tier you can earn depends on how sensitive the data is.
Mandatory for public agencies
No stamp, and a government office can't rent it. A precondition for any public-sector cloud migration.
CSPM
The door-checking that continues after the stamp. CSAP is a one-time certification; CSPM is the ongoing check afterward. → door-checking the rented warehouse
Cloud
The rented warehouse. A warehouse kept outside the castle and rented for use.
Physical security
The safety of the warehouse building itself. One of the items on the inspector's checklist.
Compliance
The broader idea of being checked and confirmed. CSAP is a compliance program specific to the cloud. → the inspector from the neighboring kingdom
