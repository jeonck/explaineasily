What is AI Ethics & Governance?

AI ethics and governance, told as a story about a shared grading charter that every inspection station follows — principles and accountability together.

Every station grades by its own rule.The same produce passes at one station and fails at another.

Without a written standard, nobody can explain it.Why the grade came out that way, and who's responsible, stays a mystery.

A shared charter every station follows — principles and accountability together.Not one sorter's private rule — the frame the whole station operates inside.

Keep it fairno bias by produce type
Make it explainablerecording why a grade was given
Fix accountabilitywho owns a bad call
Allow human reviewopening a path to appeal

The charter spells out the appeal process in concrete steps.Review only works if the reason for the grade was recorded in the first place.

Having a charter doesn't help if nobody follows it.This page takes the ethics/compliance angle; the kitchen world's page takes the security-ops angle.

In one breath

AI ethics & governance = a shared grading charter spelling out fairness, explainability, and accountability for every station.

AI ethics and governance is the policy and normative framework ensuring fairness, transparency (explainability), accountability, and a right to human review throughout how an organization builds and operates AI. In Korean PE exams it's discussed alongside the OECD AI Principles, the EU AI Act, and the ethics/trustworthiness requirements of Korea's AI Basic Act. Unlike security-operations governance focused on incident response, this is the wider frame of social acceptability, regulatory compliance, and stakeholder trust.
