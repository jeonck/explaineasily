What is a VLAN?

Virtual LANs, told as a story about a hallway split up with colored ribbons.

One hallway, and everyone mixes.A guest laptop can knock on the vault door — exactly what the hallway watcher worries about.

Walls mean building whole new hallways.Three new hallways is expensive and slow.

A VLAN splits the hallway with colored ribbons.Ribbons instead of walls. Blue talks only to blue. One hallway, three neighborhoods.

In one breath

A VLAN = one hallway split into several with colored ribbons. Same color talks to same color; crossing colors means a gatekeeper.

Virtual LAN, IEEE 802.1Q. Ribbons are tags numbered 1 to 4094. Cut the ribbons finer and you get micro-segmentation — the hallway version of the castle that always asks.

When grown-ups say it

Tag · VLAN ID
The ribbon's color. Numbered 1 to 4094. The socket, not the person, hands out the ribbon.
Broadcast domain
How far a shout carries. Shout, and only your color hears.
Access port
A one-color socket. Plug in, get that color's ribbon. → the gatekeeper at every socket
Trunk
The passage every color shares. One passage between buildings, ribbons on, everyone together.
Inter-VLAN routing
The gatekeeper between colors. Green to red passes here. → the wall with many doors
VLAN hopping
Swapping ribbons. A thief's trick that exploits the ribbon being only a promise.
Native VLAN
How the ribbonless are treated. No ribbon? You count as this color. Thieves love it.
Micro-segmentation
Ribbons cut finer. Down to one room, one device. → the castle that always asks
