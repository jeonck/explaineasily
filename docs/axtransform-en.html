In one breath

AX = not bolting on one AI camera, but redesigning the way of working itself, from the ground up, around AI.

AX (AI Transformation) goes beyond bolting AI tools onto existing work — digitization, automation, DX — to redesign the work process, the organizational structure, and the decision-making itself, with AI placed at the center. Where DX (digital transformation) focuses on doing the same work faster and more accurately, AX starts from a different question: how should the work be structured in the first place? Reassigning human roles and managing change turn out to matter more than the technology itself, and before restructuring, principles and procedures like AI ethics/governance and an AI risk management framework need to already be in place.

When grown-ups say it

AX (AI transformation)
Redesigning the whole station from the ground up. Entirely different from just bolting on one camera.
DX (digital transformation)
Doing the same work faster, through screens and automation. AX isn't the next step after DX — it starts from a different question.
Process redesign
Redrawing the steps of the work itself. The core work of AX.
Change management
Helping people get used to their new roles. Often the harder part — harder than the technology itself.
AI ethics & governance
The charter that needs to exist before any restructuring. The principle AX leans on. → The Grading Charter Every Station Shares
AI risk management framework
The operations map that keeps running even while the structure changes. Doesn't pause just because AX is underway. → The Operations Map That Walks the Whole Station
Exception handling
What's left for a person to do, after AX. Not everything — just the rare cases that still need a person.
Data-driven operations
Seeing and optimizing the whole station through its numbers. The operating style AX is aiming toward.
