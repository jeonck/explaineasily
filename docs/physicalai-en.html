In one breath

Physical AI = an AI that already sees and judges, with sensing and physical movement added on top.

Physical AI perceives its surroundings through cameras and sensors, then carries that judgment out through real motors and actuators as physical action — moving, manipulating, modulating force. It loops through perception, decision, and action in real time, and unlike a screen-bound AI, it must also handle the laws of physics — weight, friction, latency — alongside real-time safety measures like emergency stops and force limits. Robotics, autonomous driving, and industrial robot arms are classic examples, and because they so often touch life and safety directly, they frequently brush up against high-impact AI regulation.

When grown-ups say it

Physical AI

Perception-decision-action loop
Seeing, deciding, and moving — on an unbroken repeat. The most basic operating structure of physical AI.
CNN
The seeing eye physical AI borrows. The very same method used for screen-bound classification. → The Eye That Scans Through a Small Window
Actuator
The motor that turns a decision into real movement. The part that actually moves the gripper or limb.
Force limiting & e-stop
A safeguard that stops everything the instant it grips or hits too hard. The core safeguard against a mistake that can't be undone.
High-impact AI
The classification for AI that touches life and safety directly. An AI with a body is especially likely to fall into this category. → The Minimum Grading Rule the Country Itself Sets
Simulation training
Practicing in a fake environment before ever bursting a real piece of produce. Especially important when physical AI's mistakes are this costly.
