What is a Functional Safety Standard (ISO 26262, IEC 61508)?

Functional safety standards, told as a story about the student council giving every field's own claim of "it's safe" one shared grading scale and third-party certification.

Every field has its own idea of what "safe" means.Different words for "safe" can't be compared against each other.

Without a shared standard, you can't compare or trust any of it.Without a shared standard, only an accident reveals the truth.

A functional safety standard is a shared rulebook that grades risk and mandates matching safeguards.Gives every field's own "it's safe" the same grading scale.

In one breath

Functional safety standards = safety rulebooks built on the common base, IEC 61508, then rewritten and specialized for a domain like automobiles (ISO 26262), using risk grades (SIL/ASIL).

IEC 61508 is a general international standard covering functional safety of electrical, electronic, and programmable electronic systems, grading risk with SIL (Safety Integrity Level) 1 through 4. ISO 26262 specializes this for the automotive industry, using ASIL (Automotive Safety Integrity Level) A through D and setting requirements across the vehicle's entire lifecycle — planning, design, verification, production, and decommissioning. Higher grades demand stricter development and verification processes, plus independent third-party certification.

When grown-ups say it

Functional Safety Standard
A shared rulebook that grades risk. Rewritten into a domain-specific version for each field.
IEC 61508
The base standard every field starts from. Covers electrical and electronic systems broadly.
ISO 26262
The standard specialized for automobiles. Rewrites IEC 61508 specifically for cars.
SIL (Safety Integrity Level)
A risk-based grade, 1 through 4. Used by IEC 61508 and several industry-specific standards.
ASIL (Automotive Safety Integrity Level)
The automotive-specific grade, A through D. A grading scheme unique to ISO 26262.
Third-Party Certification
Not declaring safety yourself — getting it checked from outside. Independently verifies the standard was actually followed.
