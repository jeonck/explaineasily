What is AI Privacy?

AI privacy, told as a story about what happens when you paste the whole guest list into a rented parrot — and how to mask the secret beans and get a promise.

The clerk pasted the whole guest list into the parrot.Handy — but where do these beans go? Do they become books at the parrot school?

A rented parrot lives in someone else's house — beans can stay, become books, and leak into other answers.Beans given to a rented parrot land on someone else's tray. If that house keeps them or uses them as the next school's books, they can one day pop out in another guest's answer.

AI privacy is masking the secret beans or keeping them for our own parrot — and getting a promise they won't become books.Secret beans are personal data — names, phones, cards — and company secrets. Mask them before pasting, and get the promise in a contract.

In one breath

AI privacy = masking the secret beans (personal data, company secrets) or keeping them for our own parrot, and getting a promise they won't become books. Masked pieces can still add up and read books can't be unread, so the best move is not handing them over at all.

Handling personal data and confidential information in prompts, chat logs and fine-tuning data. The tools are masking and pseudonymization before input, enterprise contracts (DPAs) fixing training opt-out, retention periods and data region, and self-hosting (open weights). Pseudonymized data can still be re-identified, and information already trained in is hard to remove.

When grown-ups say it

Data privacy
Keeping the secret beans safe. The principles live in the castle. → privacy
Training opt-out
The promise: not the next book. Check the terms or settings — some places default to using it.
Data retention policy
How many days before the beans are deleted. 30 days? Forever? Put it in the contract. → retention
Masking · Pseudonymization
Name → tag. The tag table stays home. → masking
Data residency
The country the beans stay in. Laws differ by country, so the contract says which region.
Enterprise agreement · DPA
One contract scroll. One page promising no training, a retention period, a region, and notice if something goes wrong.
Memory
The parrot's notebook. Secret beans written in the notebook are the same problem. → the parrot's notebook
Open weights
Our own parrot. Secret beans never leave home. → our own parrot vs a rented one
