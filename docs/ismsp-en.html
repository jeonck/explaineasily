In one breath

ISMS/ISMS-P = the certification plaque a government inspector hangs after checking a castle's whole management system and protection measures. ISMS-P also checks the entire personal-data lifecycle.

ISMS (Information Security Management System) and ISMS-P (adding Personal Information protection) are statutory certifications in Korea, audited by bodies such as KISA. Businesses past a certain size must obtain it, with an annual follow-up review after initial certification. ISMS covers the overall security management system; ISMS-P additionally reviews requirements across the full personal-data lifecycle, from collection to destruction.

When grown-ups say it

ISMS
Security management system certification. Reviews the management system and the protection measures.
ISMS-P
ISMS plus the personal-data lifecycle. Also reviews collecting, using, and destroying guest info, start to finish.
Certification body (KISA 등)
The office that sends the inspector. Bodies such as KISA conduct the review.
ISMS management process
Policy, organization, risk assessment. Setting the whole castle's rules and who owns what. → the castle rule scroll
Protection requirements
Each lockdown item. Specific items like access control and encryption.
Follow-up review
Re-checked every year. Initial certification isn't the end — it's reviewed annually.
Mandatory scope
Mandatory past a certain size. Businesses over revenue or user thresholds must obtain it.
Compliance
The inspector checks the evidence. ISMS-P is also proven with evidence. → the inspector from next door
