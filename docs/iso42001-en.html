In one breath

ISO/IEC 42001 = the international standard that checks an organization using AI has risk management, explainability, and human oversight in place, and gives it a certification stamp.

ISO/IEC 42001:2023 is the world's first international standard for an AI Management System (AIMS). It follows the same PDCA structure as ISO 27001 (information security management) while adding requirements specific to AI — risk, explainability, data quality, and human oversight. Certification proves an organization has a system for managing AI responsibly — not that every decision the AI makes is correct.

When grown-ups say it

ISO/IEC 42001
The stamp certifying AI is managed well. The world's first AI management system standard.
AI Management System
The whole system for managing AI. Covers everything from risk management to ongoing improvement.
PDCA (Plan-Do-Check-Act)
The cycle of plan, do, check, and fix. The common backbone of ISO management-system standards.
AI Risk Management
Spotting risks like bias or malfunction in advance. A core activity 42001 requires.
Explainability
Being able to explain why a result came out that way. No record means no explanation either.
Human Oversight
Not letting AI make the final call alone. A human-review step is always kept in place.
ISO/IEC 27001
The certification for information-security management. The earlier standard this one is structured after. → the inspector from the neighboring kingdom
AI Ethics & Governance
The day-to-day principles for running AI responsibly. Unlike this standard's certification frame, it covers the operating principles themselves. → the shared grading charter
