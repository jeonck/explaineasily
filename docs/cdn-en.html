What is a CDN?

Content Delivery Network, told as a story about copy depots in every town.

Everyone travels to the castle for its picture books.Far towns wait a long time, and the castle gate is jammed.

Everyone wants the same books.The castle fetches the same thing over and over. What a waste.

A CDN puts a copy depot in every town.You get it from the nearest depot. If it's missing, one trip to the castle — then it's in the depot for good.

Copiesoriginals at the castle, copies in depots
The nearest depotthe nearer, the faster
Expirystale copies get tossed
The castle restsquiet even on busy days

When the castle changes a picture, the depots swap their copies.When a copy expires, or the castle says toss it, a fresh one comes down.

Your own letters never go in the depot.Only things everyone reads get copied; your own letters mean a trip to the castle. And until a copy is swapped, you may see the old one.

In one breath

A CDN = copies of the castle's picture books in a depot in every town, so everyone gets them from nearby.

Content Delivery Network. Photos, videos and picture books — things everyone reads — come from the depot; your own letters come from the castle. Cloudflare, Akamai, AWS CloudFront. Same idea as the hubs all over town (SASE), but for picture books instead of security.

When grown-ups say it

Cache
The copy. The book left in the depot. → the same as the runner's basket
Edge · PoP
The town depot. A depot placed near people. → the same spot as the hubs all over town
Origin
The castle. Where the original lives. Visited only when the depot lacks something.
TTL (time to live)
The expiry date. How long a copy stays. Past it, a fresh one is fetched.
Cache hit · miss
In the depot · not in the depot. There? Right away. Not there? One trip to the castle.
Purge
Toss the old edition. The castle clearing depot copies before they expire.
Static · dynamic
Picture books · my letters. Shared things get copied; things meant just for you don't.
Origin shielding
On busy days the depots take the crowd. The castle gate stays quiet. Even a mob of thieves (DDoS) hits the depots first.
