What is ESG Management and IT?

ESG management and IT's role in it, told as a story about a tour that must prove its environmental, social, and governance record, not just a good show.

In one breath

ESG management and IT = minding environment, society, and governance alongside the show's success — with IT gathering the proof as data.

ESG management builds Environmental, Social, and Governance concerns into how a business runs. IT's role is collecting and integrating data — carbon emissions, work records, accounting — into dashboards and sustainability reports. Promotion without real measurement behind it draws criticism as greenwashing.

When grown-ups say it

Environment
Environmental impact like travel and waste. Carbon-footprint measurement is the classic example.
Social
How staff and audience are treated and kept safe. Includes working conditions and safety logs.
Governance
Transparent accounting and decision-making. Audit and reporting structure is the core of it.
Greenwashing
Claiming eco-friendliness without data to back it. Without proof, it gets caught fast.
Sustainability Report
The document that discloses ESG data. Filled in with data IT has gathered.
Data Dashboard
A single screen showing scattered ESG numbers at a glance. IT automatically gathers and displays it.
IT Investment Evaluation
A scorecard that also weighs intangibles like brand. ESG performance is one kind of intangible benefit too. → the scorecard after the show
Ops Performance Mgmt
Continuing to measure metrics after the show. ESG metrics get tracked through ops too. → the ops room after the show
