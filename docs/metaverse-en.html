What is the Metaverse?

Metaverse, told as a story about a virtual pavilion where avatars gather together, instead of a flat video call.

We're all here, but it still feels like we're apart.A flat screen has no sense of 'being together.'

Everyone's stuck in their own little box — not a shared space.So it never quite feels immersive.

Metaverse is an internet where avatars share the same virtual space together.Everyone's gathered in one space, as avatars.

Represent myself as an avatara stand-in for the real me
Share a 3D spacetogether in the same place
Interact in real timevoice and motion, live
Join a virtual economybuy and sell items

All four together make it feel like 'being there.'Missing even one, and it's just another screen.

Wear it too long and it's dizzying, and the gear is pricey.Different metaverses still don't talk to each other.

In one breath

The metaverse is an internet that gives you the feeling of being together in the same virtual space, as an avatar — complete only with a shared space, real-time interaction, and a virtual economy together.

The metaverse is an extended internet environment where users, through avatars, interact in real time in immersive 3D virtual spaces and trade virtual goods. VR/AR headsets are just one way to enter it, and a lack of interoperability between different metaverse platforms, plus fatigue from extended wear, remain unresolved challenges.

When grown-ups say it

Metaverse
The virtual space avatars share together. Needs space, interaction, and an economy, all together.
Avatar
A virtual stand-in for me. Represents me inside the metaverse.
Virtual Reality
The headset technology for entering a virtual world. One way to enter the metaverse. → the VR booth
Digital Twin
A twin that copies reality exactly. A twin copies reality; the metaverse can invent new worlds too. → the Digital Twin booth
Virtual Economy
Trading items inside the virtual world. Sometimes connects to real money.
Interoperability
How well different metaverses talk to each other. Still fairly low right now.
Immersion
The feeling of really being there. The very thing a flat screen can't give.
Immersive Content
Video and spatial content built to create immersion. Used in both metaverse and VR.
