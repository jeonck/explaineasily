What is an APT?

Advanced Persistent Threat, told as a story about a gang with a royal sponsor that hides for months and wants just one thing: the crown.

An ordinary thief takes one night. This thief has been quiet for months.An ordinary thief opens a chest and the bell rings. This one never rang the bell — and is already inside.

They disguise themselves, wipe their footprints, and move one page a day.Nothing is left in the log book, and they never move big enough to ring a bell. So nobody notices.

An APT is a persistent gang of thieves with a country behind them.A foreign king pays the bill. The target is one crown. They may take all the time they need. That's what makes them different from a one-night thief.

One step a month. Five months later they're in the crown room.A fake letter gets them into one room. There they find the next room's key, and move on. Five months inside, and nobody knew.

In one breath

APT = a persistent gang with a country behind it that wants one crown, hides for months, wipes its footprints, and moves a little at a time.

Advanced Persistent Threat: a well-funded attack group, often state-sponsored, picks a specific target and stays hidden inside for a long time, moving quietly and stealing information. Catching one takes threat intelligence, TTP analysis, and threat hunting together.

When grown-ups say it

APT (Advanced Persistent Threat)
The gang that waits for months. A sponsor, one target, a long time. Not a one-night thief.
State-sponsored actor
A foreign king pays. So money and time are plentiful. Catch one, and the next gang comes.
Dwell time
From the day they got in to the day they were found. Months is common. Shrinking it is what hunting is for.
Lateral movement
Finding the next room's key from inside one room. Cook's room → next room → crown room. This is what the corridor guard watches for.
Persistence
A back door for getting back in. Block one door and they return through another they dug earlier.
Exfiltration
Sneaking papers out of the castle. All at once would ring the bell, so a little at a time. This is what the red stamp guards against.
Threat group names (APT28 …)
A number and a nickname for each gang. Names like APT28 or Lazarus. The thief encyclopedia lists each gang's habits.
Kill chain
The steps from letter to crown. Cut any step and the whole thing fails. → the seven steps of a thief
