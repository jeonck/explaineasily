What are Circuit & Packet Switching?

Circuit and packet switching, told as a story about choosing between reserving a whole road for a call, or splitting an envelope into pieces to share the road.

While they talk, the whole road is reserved just for the two of them.Even during silence, that road stays reserved, unused.

More people pushes the dedicated-road method to its limit.The roads are limited, but the number of users keeps growing.

Packet switching = splitting the envelope into pieces, then reassembling them.No one hogs a road, so many people share it at once.

The two methods trade off different things.Circuit switching is steady; packet switching is efficient.

Packet switching can have pieces arrive out of order.Registered mail (TCP) is how packet switching still fixes the order on top.

In one breath

Circuit switching = reserving one whole road for the duration of a call. Packet switching = splitting into pieces, sending them separately, and reassembling them.

Circuit switching dedicates a line exclusively until the session ends — stable quality, but wasteful of resources (the classic phone network). Packet switching splits data into packets, sends them over a shared line, and reassembles them in order at the destination (the classic internet). It uses resources efficiently, but can suffer congestion, delay, and out-of-order arrival.

When grown-ups say it

Circuit Switching
The dedicated road reserved for the whole call. The classic telephone-network method.
Packet Switching
Splitting the envelope into pieces to send separately. The basic method of today's internet.
Packet
Each individual piece of the split envelope. Carries a sequence number for reassembly.
Transport Layer (TCP/UDP)
The stage that decides registered mail or plain. Handles order and reliability on top of packet switching. → the stage that splits ports and picks reliability
OSI 7 Layers
The whole delivery process split into 7 stages. Switching corresponds to specific stages in this process. → the delivery process split into 7 stages
Modbus
A simple note rule used on factory floors. This rule, too, ultimately rides on one of these switching methods. → the note rule where the clerk asks one at a time
Ad-hoc Routing
Finding the way among themselves, with no fixed post office. An extreme case of packets finding a fresh path each time. → how carriers find the way among themselves
5G Network Slicing
The technology that splits the road into situational lanes. Carves out dedicated lanes, echoing circuit switching's idea. → the network with a lane for every need
