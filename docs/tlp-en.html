What is TLP?

The Traffic Light Protocol, told as a story about secret letters.

Every secret letter gets a colored sticker.It answers: "how far can I pass this on?"

Each color is a bigger circle.Red is just me. Clear is the whole world.

REDonly who got it
AMBERinside our castle
GREENour village
CLEARanyone

Keep the promise, and you'll hear the next secret.Spread it, and your friend stops telling you things.

In one breath

TLP = a color that says how far a secret may travel.

Traffic Light Protocol. The current version is 2.0 (2022): WHITE became CLEAR, and AMBER+STRICT was added.

When grown-ups say it

TLP:RED
Recipients only. Heard it in a meeting? Stays in that room.
TLP:AMBER
Inside our organization. Colleagues and clients who need it.
TLP:AMBER+STRICT
Our organization only. Not even clients.
TLP:GREEN
Our community. Peers in the same field. Never the open internet.
TLP:CLEAR
Anyone. Free to publish. Used to be called WHITE.
FIRST
Who wrote the rules. A global forum of security response teams. Published TLP 2.0.
