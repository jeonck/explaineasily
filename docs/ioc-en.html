Explain like I'm five

What is an IOC?

Indicators of Compromise, told as a story about footprints.

Someone was here.They left footprints.

Clues come in a few shapes.Where they came from, and what they left behind.

A strange addressan IP address
A strange sendera domain or URL
A fingerprint on a thinga file hash

You share the footprint with friends.What one castle saw, every castle can look for.

But new shoes make a new footprint.So footprints alone aren't enough. You need their habits (TTPs) too.

In one breath

IOC = a trace the bad guy left. If you see it, they were already here.

An indicator doesn't stop the break-in; it helps you spot one that happened. Shoes are cheap to change, so indicators go stale fast.

When grown-ups say it

File hash
A thing's fingerprint. A long number unique to one file. Change one byte and the fingerprint changes.
IP address
A house address. Where the bad guy's computer lives.
Domain
A house name. A name used instead of the address. Buying a new one takes hours.
Threat feed
A stack of footprint photos. Traces other castles saw, arriving all day.
False positive
Our own dog's paw print. Looked like the burglar's, wasn't.
