What are Personal-Data Safeguard Measures and the Internal Management Plan?

Personal-data safeguard measures and the internal management plan, told as a story about a rulebook written just for the guest-info storeroom.

The guest-info storeroom gets treated like any other room.It swings wildly depending on who's in charge.

A leak hurts every single guest, one by one.That's why it has to be locked down far more strictly than any ordinary room.

The internal management plan is a rulebook written just for the guest-info storeroom.More detailed and stricter than the castle's general rule scroll.

Access-rights managementa visitor log, kept to the minimum
Access-log retentionwho came in, and when

Physical security, antivirusthe lock, and the bug-blocker

It's a separate rulebook, apart from the castle's general rules.Its items are legal requirements set by the Personal Information Protection Act's enforcement decree and the safeguard-measures notice.

A rulebook with nothing behind it is worthless.Words on a page are just paper — real access logs are what prove it. The inspector always checks the evidence first.

In one breath

An internal management plan = a lockdown rulebook written just for the guest-info storeroom. It spells out access rights, access logs, encryption, and physical security in detail.

Safeguard measures for personal data are the technical, managerial, and physical protection standards set by the enforcement decree of Korea's Personal Information Protection Act and its related notices. The internal management plan is the document that spells out how an organization actually meets those standards — covering access-rights management, access-log retention, encryption, antivirus, and physical security. Establishing and enforcing it is mandatory for personal-data handlers above a certain size.

When grown-ups say it

Internal management plan
The storeroom's own rulebook document. A document any organization handling guest info must actually draw up.
Safeguard-measures notice
The detailed items set by law. A notice from Korea's Personal Information Protection Commission, setting technical, managerial, and physical measures.
Access-rights management
The visitor log. Decides and records who may enter, kept to the minimum.
Access-log retention and review
The log of who came in, and when. Kept for a set period (usually one to two years) and reviewed regularly.
Encrypting unique identifiers
Mandatory for things like ID numbers. Locked again even after it's inside the castle. → the letter nobody can read without the key
Chief Privacy Officer (CPO)
The officer responsible for the storeroom. The person responsible for drawing up and enforcing the plan.
Castle-wide security policy
The general rules. The internal management plan is its guest-info-only version. → the castle rule scroll
Compliance
The inspector checks the evidence. A plan with no records behind it fails inspection. → the inspector from next door
