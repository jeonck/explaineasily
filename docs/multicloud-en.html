What is Multi-Cloud?

Multi-cloud, told as a strategy of moving into several buildings at once instead of depending on just one.

Relying on just one building for everything feels risky.It means betting everything on a single building.

Trouble in one building, and everything stops together.Because there was only one place to depend on.

Multi-cloud = one company moves into several buildings at once.A strategy to avoid depending on any one building.

Prepares for disasterif one goes down, another covers
Compares the rentprice differs building to building
Meets local rulessome data must stay in a specific region

There are four reasons to split across buildings.Which reason matters most differs company to company.

Different rules per building make management harder.Staff and cost multiply with every extra building.

In one breath

Multi-cloud is a strategy where one company moves into several cloud "buildings" at once, to reduce the risk of depending on just one.

A strategy of using multiple cloud providers (two or more CSPs) at the same time. The goals are spreading risk against one vendor's outage or price hikes, using each provider's particular strengths, and meeting regional or regulatory requirements. The trade-off is that different providers have different APIs and operational models, so management complexity and cost rise together.

When grown-ups say it

Multi-cloud
The strategy of moving into several buildings at once. Reduces the risk of depending on just one.
Vendor lock-in
Being fit so tightly to one building's way that you can't move. Exactly the risk multi-cloud tries to reduce.
Disaster recovery
Another building covering when one goes down. One of the biggest reasons to split across buildings.
Data sovereignty / regional rules
The rule that some data must stay in a building in a specific region. Especially important in the public sector.
Hybrid cloud
Using your own building together with a shared one. Often confused with multi-cloud, but the axis differs — whether your own building is included.
Public-sector cloud adoption
The rule: move into a certified building, at the right grade. Public agencies sometimes use multi-cloud for redundancy too. → only into a certified building
Cloud service model
Bare shell / interior done / move-in ready. Any building is rented as one of these three models. → pick one to rent
API
Each building's own "how to apply for a lease" process. Differs by building, which is what makes multi-cloud management complex.
