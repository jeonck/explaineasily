What is a Bug Bounty?

Bug bounties, told as a story about a poster on the castle gate: cracks wanted, reward offered.

The castle keeps growing, but only two people look for cracks.One carpenter and a hired thief who comes once a year. Every new tower brings new cracks.

The village has hundreds of eyes. But nowhere to tell.When someone who spots a crack has nowhere to tell, they walk on — or sell it at the bad market.

A bug bounty is one poster on the castle gate.Anyone may look for cracks. Tell us quietly with a note, and once confirmed you're rewarded by the size of the crack. Just break nothing, and open no chests.

A note comes in: confirm it, pay it, board it up.The guard room checks whether it's real and whether it's already known. It stays secret until fixed — after that, the finder may brag.

Now there are hundreds of eyes around the castle.The ones who spot a crack before the thief are on our side now. A pentest is one person once; a bounty is everyone, always.

In one breath

Bug bounty = a poster on the gate — anyone who finds a crack and sends a quiet note gets a reward by size once confirmed, and the crack gets boarded up.

A public program where any outside researcher can find vulnerabilities, report them under set rules, and be paid by severity once verified.

When grown-ups say it

Bug bounty
The reward poster. One reward per crack; the bigger the crack, the bigger the reward.
Vulnerability disclosure policy
The poster without a reward. A poster that only says where to tell. No money, but now there's somewhere to go.
Scope
Where you may look. The main keep, yes; the king's bedroom, no. Written on the poster in advance.
Coordinated disclosure
Secret until fixed. Us first, the village after the board goes up. → the hole nobody knows
Severity
Size of the crack. A crack a cat fits through and a crack a thief walks through pay differently.
Duplicate
A note we already have. Only the first note about a crack gets the reward.
Bounty platform
The village noticeboard keeper. Puts up the poster, collects the notes, and hands over the rewards.
Pentest
One hired thief. Set time, set person; a bounty is the opposite. → the thief we hired
