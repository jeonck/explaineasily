What is IS Operations Performance Management?

IS operations performance management, told as a story about an ops room that keeps watching the ticketing and sound systems long after the show ends.

The show is over, but the ticket system still has to keep running.It isn't "build it and done" — there's an after, too.

Unwatched, it quietly gets worse over time.Right after launch and months later are different — you only know by keeping watch.

Ops performance management is keeping watch on the system even after the tour ends.The show ends, but the watching doesn't.

Measure uptimecheck it hasn't gone down
Measure latencycheck it hasn't slowed down
Measure satisfactionlisten for complaints
Improve, then repeatfeed it into the next tour

Ops keeps watching these four things.These four keep getting measured even after the tour ends.

The question investment evaluation first asked keeps being answered through ops performance management.One show ends, but the asking and answering keeps going.

In one breath

IS operations performance management = continuing to measure and improve uptime, latency, and satisfaction, even after the project (show) ends.

IS operations performance management continuously measures metrics like availability, latency, and user satisfaction, feeding results back into improvement through a PDCA (Plan-Do-Check-Act) cycle. If IT investment evaluation asks its question once, after the project ends, operations performance management is the ongoing process of continuing to answer it.

When grown-ups say it

Availability/Uptime
The share of time the system stays up. Cut down by incidents like outages.
Latency
How fast the system responds to a request. Easy to miss as it slowly degrades.
User Satisfaction
Whether the audience used it without friction. Measured via surveys and complaint counts.
PDCA
The loop of Plan, Do, Check, Act. Ops improvement keeps turning this loop.
SLA/SLM
The guarantee ticket agreed with the sponsor, and managing it. Ops metrics become the evidence that the promise was kept. → the ticket that guarantees the show
IT Investment Evaluation
The scorecard checking if the investment paid off. Ops data keeps supplying the answer to that question. → the scorecard after the show
BCP
The plan that keeps the show going through a disaster. Whether BCP had to trigger is logged in ops records too. → the tour that doesn't stop for a blackout
IS Audit/PMO
A role that checks everything from the outside. Ops metrics are among the things audit checks too. → the last checker before publication
