What is Software/Functional Safety?

Software and functional safety, told as a story about putting a book — like a first-aid manual, where one typo can cost a life — through several layers of extra-strict verification.

A single typo in a dosage unit almost slipped through.In an ordinary book it wouldn't matter much — but this book is different.

A claim that it can be reviewed like any other book, met with a reminder that lives are at stake.Not every book can be checked at the same intensity.

A higher-risk book goes through several extra-strict layers of checking.The number of layers changes with the risk grade.

Verification doesn't bring risk down to zero.It's about lowering risk to a level you can live with.

In one breath

Software/functional safety = putting a higher-risk book through several extra-strict layers of verification.

Functional safety ensures that even if a system fails, people aren't harmed — by applying verification levels (like SIL grades) in proportion to risk. The higher the risk, the more layered safeguards are required: independent review, redundancy, real-world trials. The goal isn't perfect zero-defect — it's lowering risk to an acceptable level.

When grown-ups say it

Functional Safety
Designing so a failure doesn't hurt anyone. Requirements differ by risk grade.
Safety Integrity Level
The scale that grades risk. A higher level demands stricter verification.
Independent Verification
A separate team checking it again. One side's blind spot gets caught by the other.
Redundancy
A structure where one failing still leaves another standing. Works on a similar principle to cross-checking.
Risk Analysis
Gauging in advance which books carry more risk. Done before grading risk.
White-box/Black-box Testing
Checking methods split by looking inside or only outside. The higher the risk, the deeper and more often both get done. → looking inside, or just reading the outside
IS Audit/PMO
The last checker before publication. The higher the risk grade, the stricter the audit gets too. → the last checker before publication
Safety Case
The dossier gathering evidence that it's safe enough. Compiles verification records to explain to outsiders.
