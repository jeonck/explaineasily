What is an AI Risk Management Framework?

An AI risk management framework, told as a story about an operations map that walks the whole station routinely — not just after something breaks.

Only after an accident hits does anyone scramble to react.Nobody had looked ahead at the risk beforehand.

Risk is scattered everywhere — from the seed to outside farms.The same kind of risk gets handled differently depending on where it's found.

A four-stage map that walks the whole station.After one loop, it starts over — this map never really finishes.

① Governsetting principles and accountability first
② Mapfinding where risk hides
③ Measuresizing it and tracking it
④ Managereducing it, then looking again

Each of the four stages has its own concrete job.Principles without execution are just a map hanging on the wall.

The framework doesn't make risk disappear entirely.Ethics principles and the legal baseline both move along this same map.

In one breath

An AI risk management framework = an operations map that walks the whole station through govern, map, measure, and manage.

An AI risk management framework identifies (Map), measures (Measure), and mitigates (Manage) the risks of an AI system, run repeatedly inside an organization-wide accountability structure (Govern). The US NIST AI RMF's four functions — Govern, Map, Measure, Manage — are the best-known model, and the risk-assessment and safety-assurance requirements in Korea's AI Basic Act follow the same flow. It isn't a one-time pass — it loops again every time the model or its environment changes.

When grown-ups say it

AI risk management framework
The operations map that walks the whole station. It doesn't finish after one loop — it keeps going.
NIST AI RMF
The four functions: govern, map, measure, manage. The most widely used framework in this field.
AI ethics & governance
The charter the govern stage leans on. The starting principle this map is built on. → The Grading Charter Every Station Shares
Risk assessment
A term that bundles together the map and measure stages. Answers both where the risk is and how big it is.
Mitigation
The concrete action applied in the manage stage. The execution step that shrinks a measured risk.
AI Basic Act
The minimum risk-management duty the law itself enforces. High-impact AI is legally required to walk this same map.
Residual risk
The risk still left after managing it. The part that admits risk never quite reaches zero.
Supply-chain risk
Risk arriving from outside farms. Why the map stage has to look past the station's own fence.
