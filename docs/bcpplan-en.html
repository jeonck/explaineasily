What is BCP (Business Continuity Plan)?

BCP, told as a story about a plan made in advance to keep the core show going even when the venue loses power.

The venue suddenly loses power.The whole show grinds to a halt.

Without a plan ready, panic sets in and recovery drags on.Even what to save first isn't obvious in the moment.

BCP is a plan made in advance to keep the core show going, even in an emergency.Even if the power goes out, this show doesn't stop.

Identify the coredecide what absolutely must keep running
Prepare an alternativea backup, like a standby generator
Set a target recovery timeRTO — how fast it must come back
Run drillsonly practicing it proves it works

BCP covers the whole business; DRP covers the broken system.System recovery (DRP) is one piece of keeping the business going (BCP).

Left in a drawer, it's useless.A plan isn't meant for the drawer — drills keep it alive.

In one breath

BCP = a plan made in advance to keep core operations (the show) running through a major disruption like a blackout — and checked with drills.

BCP (Business Continuity Plan) identifies core operations, defines a target recovery time (RTO) and backup measures, and is checked for real effectiveness through regular drills. DRP (Disaster Recovery Plan), a related concept, focuses on IT system recovery and is one component within BCP.

When grown-ups say it

Core Business Identification
Picking the show that absolutely must go on. You can't save everything at once.
Alternative Resource
A backup, like a standby generator. Keeps the core operation running.
Recovery Time Objective
The target time to be back up. Shorter targets cost more to achieve.
BCP Drill
Actually practicing the plan. An untested plan is just paper in a drawer.
DRP
The plan for recovering a broken system. Covered in depth in another world (the castle). → how to open the market the morning after the fire
Project Risk Mgmt
Preparing for surprises, like carrying an umbrella. BCP is the preparation for the biggest risks among them. → the umbrella you carry even on a dry day
Ops Performance Mgmt
Watching whether systems keep running well afterward. Whether BCP had to trigger is tracked as an ops metric too. → the ops room after the show
ESG Management
Management that also minds safety and treatment. It touches the 'S' (social) axis of ESG too. → the tour that minds environment, society, and transparency too
