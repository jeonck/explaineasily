What is Multimodal?

Multimodal models, told as a story about a parrot that turns pictures and sounds into beans too.

The guest holds up a receipt photo. A words-only parrot cannot see it.Tables, charts, photos — all the same. The parrot might as well be blind.

Because the parrot was a bird that only ate beans. A picture is not a bean.The beak is shaped for beans. The numbers in the photo never become beans, so the parrot does not even know they are there.

Multimodal is a parrot that turns pictures and sounds into beans too.One photo becomes hundreds of beans. Then it can continue them just like word beans — as if it were looking at the photo.

In one breath

Multimodal = a parrot that turns pictures and sounds into beans too. One photo is hundreds of beans, so the tray fills fast, and a person checks small print and digits.

A model that takes images, audio, or video as input (sometimes output) besides text. Images become image tokens patch by patch and use a lot of the context window; speech becomes tokens through STT. Image generation is usually a separate diffusion model.

When grown-ups say it

Multimodal
The parrot that sees pictures. Words, pictures, and sounds all become beans for one parrot.
Vision model
The picture beak. The part that turns pictures into beans. It sits attached to the big parrot.
Image tokens
Picture beans. One photo is hundreds to thousands of beans, so the tray fills fast. → the tray
Speech-to-text · text-to-speech
Sound → beans, beans → sound. STT turns speech into beans; TTS turns beans back into speech.
Difference from OCR
Pulling out letters vs eating the whole picture. OCR peels off only the letters. Multimodal reads letters, shapes, and layout together.
Document understanding
Reading tables, receipts, contracts. It reads where each cell sits. Still, a person checks the numbers.
Video input
Many pictures in a row. A video becomes many picture frames turned into beans — a lot of beans.
Image generation
The drawing parrot. Seeing and drawing are different jobs. → pulling pictures from fog
