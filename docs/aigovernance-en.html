What is AI Governance?

AI governance, told as a story about a rules board that several kitchens share — spelling out how AI gets used and handled.

Each kitchen runs its own way.And when something goes wrong, nobody knows whose fault it is.

Without a written common rule, it's different every time.Who decides and who's responsible shifts from kitchen to kitchen, moment to moment.

A kitchen rules board is the whole policy and accountability system for how AI gets used and handled.One board, and it spells out approval, checks, responsibility, and review — all in one place.

Who approves whatclearly names the decision-maker
When a person checkswhen the approval gate switches on
Who's responsible if it breakswhat follows the incident bell
How often it's reviewedthe board itself can go stale

One board ties several devices together.The approval gate, the red team, incident response, the audit log — each works on its own, but the board weaves them into one.

Having a board doesn't help if nobody follows it.Like the inspector from the neighboring kingdom, it also needs ongoing proof that it's actually being followed.

In one breath

AI governance is a single rules board that spells out how this kitchen uses and handles AI — who approves it, and who's responsible.

AI governance is the policy and accountability framework an organization uses to decide how it develops, deploys, and operates AI systems. It writes down who has approval authority, where human checks are required, who's accountable when an incident happens, and how often things get reviewed — so it applies consistently across teams and models. It's the larger frame that individual mechanisms, like a single approval gate, live inside.
