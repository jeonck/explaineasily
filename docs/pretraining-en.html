What is Pre-training?

Pre-training, told as a story about a parrot school that hides the book and makes the parrot guess the next bean, a trillion times over.

A just-hatched parrot can't continue a single bean.So where does a talking parrot come from? We said it read a mountain of books — but how does that reading work?

Reading it all takes a big school, months, and a lot of money.Thousands of calculators run day and night. The power meter maxes out and the calendar flips for months. That is why only a few places raise big parrots.

Pre-training is a school that hides the book and makes the parrot guess the next bean — nobody teaches the answers.The book itself is the answer key. When it is wrong, its inner dials get a small nudge and it moves to the next line. Do that a trillion times and you get the mountain-of-books parrot. A drill and manners lessons follow.

More books and a bigger bird guess better. And school is not the end.Grow the books, the bird and the computing together and the parrot keeps improving — in one line: bigger and more means better. After school, a short drill fixes its habits and manners lessons fix its attitude.

Fresh out of school, the parrot continues well but knows no manners and nothing recent.Doing what it is told comes from manners lessons. What happened after it closed the books is in the day it closed the books — that gap is filled by the librarian, not the school.
