What is MyData?

MyData, told as a story about the right to send your own scattered ledger pages straight to another shop yourself.

The ledger is scattered across many shops.Even the villager can't see at a glance which shop holds which of their own pages.

Every shop treats the ledger as its own.The ledger physically sits at the shop — but every line in it is the villager's own story.

MyData is the right to choose where your own ledger goes.With my consent, shops send it straight to each other. → where CBPR covers transfers between countries, MyData is the person's own request to move it.

Declared minethe ledger's content belongs to the villager

Fresh consent, every timeand can be withdrawn any time

In practice, it moves through these steps.Consent, then the request forwards, then a licensed operator carries it, then delivery. It isn't just any shop sending it on a whim.

The more convenient it gets, the more there is to protect.Only a licensed operator may handle it, and securing the delivery pipe matters most of all → privacy, the Three Data Acts amendment

In one breath

MyData = the ledger scattered across many shops is mine, so with my consent the shops can hand pages to each other directly.

MyData — the individual's right to data portability. A person can require an institution holding information about them to transmit it, on request, to the person or to another institution or licensed MyData operator the person designates, via a standardized method (an API). In Korea, financial MyData launched first under an amendment to the Credit Information Act, and only licensed MyData operators may act as the intermediary.

When grown-ups say it

MyData
The right to choose where your own ledger goes. The data subject's own right to require the transfer.
Right to data portability
The right to require the holder to transmit it. A right the villager holds, that the shop cannot refuse.
Licensed MyData operator
The licensed go-between carrying pages shop to shop. Only a licensed one may carry it.
Standardized API
The fixed delivery pipe. Delivered through one fixed pipe, not a different method at every shop.
Withdrawing consent
Taking back what was already sent. Consent to transfer can be withdrawn at any time.
Credit Information Act
The law that opened financial MyData first. One of the Three Data Acts. → the Three Data Acts amendment
Privacy
A promise that still holds after the pages are sent. MyData is consent-based transfer — it doesn't erase the duty to protect. → the visitor's name tag belongs to the visitor
CBPR
A similar rule, but for crossing kingdoms. MyData is the person's own request; CBPR is a certification between kingdoms. → CBPR
