In one breath

Negotiation-based proposal evaluation = comparing proposals on both quantitative and qualitative criteria, then negotiating the terms into a signed contract.

Proposal evaluation scores both quantitative metrics (price, schedule) and qualitative ones (skill, track record). Negotiation then finds terms both sides can accept. Preparing an alternative (BATNA) lets you negotiate from a stronger position — without pushing so hard it breaks the deal.

When grown-ups say it

Quantitative Evaluation
Comparing price and schedule as numbers. Objective, but not the whole picture.
Qualitative Evaluation
Looking at skill and track record. Captures ability that numbers miss.
BATNA
The fallback option if talks break down. Having one strengthens your position.
RFP
The open call recruiting performers. Proposal evaluation starts from this call.
WBS
The checklist that breaks show prep into pieces. Also used to gauge scope before the contract. → the checklist that breaks down show prep
SLA/SLM
The guarantee written on the ticket. Negotiated as part of the same contract terms. → the ticket that guarantees the show
IT Investment Evaluation
The scorecard checking if the investment paid off. Contract terms feed back into this evaluation too. → the scorecard after the show
IS Audit/PMO
A role that checks everything from the outside. The contracting process is also something audit checks. → the last checker before publication
