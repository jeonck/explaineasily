What are ATAM and CBAM?

A story about rehearsing several scenarios right before the press runs, to surface trade-offs while there's still time.

Right before the press runs, everyone's nervous about whether this is okay.The whole layout is already locked in — and now the nerves set in.

The problem only surfaces after everything is printed.It's already all printed — far too late to fix.

ATAM = rehearsing trade-offs against many scenarios before the press ever runs.Stakeholders gather together to find the sensitive points in a design decision.

Gather quality scenariosdurability, convertibility, speed, and more
Map to design decisionswhich choice is responsible for what
Find sensitivity and trade-off pointswhat you lose to gain the other
Record the risks earlywrite them down before the press runs

Add cost and benefit numbers to this, and it becomes CBAM.It compares, in numbers, which option actually pays off more.

Evaluation doesn't make the decision for you.It only shows what's worth weighing — the choice is still a human's to make.

In one breath

ATAM evaluates a design by rehearsing quality scenarios before the press runs, surfacing trade-offs and risks; CBAM extends this by adding cost and benefit numbers to weigh each option.

ATAM (Architecture Tradeoff Analysis Method) gathers stakeholders to write quality-attribute scenarios — performance, security, portability, and so on — then maps each scenario to the architecture's design decisions to find sensitivity points and trade-off points. CBAM (Cost Benefit Analysis Method) extends this by adding an economic analysis of each design decision's cost and benefit, helping prioritize decisions quantitatively.

When grown-ups say it

ATAM
The scenario rehearsal before the press runs. Finds trade-offs and sensitivity points.
CBAM
ATAM with cost-benefit numbers added on top. Prioritizes options with numbers.
Quality Attribute Scenario
A concrete situation like "it needs this much durability." The starting point of the evaluation.
Sensitivity Point
A spot where one decision swings quality a lot. A spot ATAM pays special attention to.
Trade-off Point
A spot where gaining one thing costs another. A sensitivity point affecting multiple qualities becomes a trade-off point.
Stakeholder
People like the editor, author, and print supervisor who gather for the evaluation. ATAM brings them all into one room.
IS Audit/PMO
Audit checks after the fact and broadly; ATAM evaluates at design time. They look at different moments. → the last checker before publication
