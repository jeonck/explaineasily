What is a Cloud Service Model?

Cloud service models (IaaS, PaaS, SaaS), told as how much of a shared office building you choose to rent.

A brand-new company needs too many things at once.Office, power, computers, software — all of it.

Preparing everything yourself means you're exhausted before you even start.Months and a fortune go in before you even open the door.

The cloud = this very shared office building.Rent exactly as much as you need.

IaaS = rent the bare shelljust power and plumbing
PaaS = interior already donejust add your own desk
SaaS = a move-in-ready officejust take the keys
Pay only for what you usemetered, not a flat rent

Who manages what changes floor by floor.The less you manage yourself, the more the building does for you.

Fit too tightly to one building, and moving later gets hard.Some still build their own, for security or regulation.

In one breath

Cloud service models are three levels of how much of the building you rent: bare shell (IaaS), interior done (PaaS), or move-in ready (SaaS).

Split into IaaS (just infrastructure like servers and networking), PaaS (adds OS and runtime so you focus only on the app), and SaaS (ready-made software you simply use). Going down the list, management burden drops and freedom drops with it. Every other page in this shared-office-building world is a story set inside this same building.

When grown-ups say it

Cloud computing
The shared office building itself. You rent exactly as much as you need.
IaaS (Infrastructure as a Service)
The bare-shell office. Provides only infrastructure like servers and networking.
PaaS (Platform as a Service)
The office with interior already done. Covers OS and runtime too, so you focus only on building the app.
SaaS (Software as a Service)
A move-in-ready office — just take the keys. You use finished software as-is.
On-premises
A building you construct yourself. Some still build their own for security or regulation.
Pay-as-you-go
Paying for what you use, instead of flat rent. Don't use it, don't pay for it.
VM and Container
The private office and the partitioned desk. Two ways to divide space on top of IaaS. → the private office and the partitioned desk
Serverless
A meeting room rented only by the hour. An extreme form where you don't even think about the shell. → the hourly meeting room
