In one breath

The Framework Act on Intelligent Informatization = the charter that sets the whole school's (nation's) direction for going digital, bakes in gap-closing and safety principles, and becomes the root that detailed laws grow from.

The Framework Act on Intelligent Informatization replaced the earlier National Informatization Basic Act. It establishes the master plan for an intelligent information society, principles for closing the digital divide, and baseline rules for information protection and data-based administration. Rather than regulating specific services itself, it serves as the upper framework law that sets direction and principles for detailed statutes like the e-Government Act, personal data protection law, and AI-related laws.

The charter covering the whole school's digital life. The upper law that detailed statutes branch from.
Master Plan for an Intelligent Information Society
The plan that sketches the big direction in advance. Redrawn every few years.
Closing the Digital Divide
Making sure no one is left behind digitally. Like looking after the tablet-less class first.
Information Protection Principle
The baseline promise to handle data and AI safely. Detailed security laws flesh this principle out.
Data-Based Administration
Deciding by data, not by gut feel. This act provides the basis for that principle.
e-Government Act
One detailed law — governs e-government services specifically. A related evaluation is covered → the board that compares schools
Personal Information Protection Act
One detailed law — specifically governs personal data. One of the detailed rules sitting under this framework act.
AI Basic Act
Another detailed law aimed squarely at AI. Handles one branch of digital transition specifically for AI.
