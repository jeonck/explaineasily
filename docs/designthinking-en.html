What is Design Thinking?

Design thinking, told as a story about an editor who stops guessing at a desk and loops through meeting readers directly instead.

Why isn't this book selling?The editor has no idea why.

Guessing alone at a desk.No one has actually met a real reader — and guesses miss, a lot.

Design thinking means meeting readers directly, and looping through building and reworking.Not desk guesses — a loop of checking things in the field.

Five stages loop around in a circle.After one loop, it goes back to empathy again.

Design thinking doesn't replace everything.It finds what to write — it doesn't replace every other step.

In one breath

Design thinking is a problem-solving method that meets readers directly instead of guessing at a desk, looping through empathize, define, ideate, prototype, and test.

Design thinking is a human-centered, iterative method that starts by empathizing with users, redefines the problem, generates many ideas, builds a cheap prototype, tests it with real users, then loops back to empathy. It focuses on finding what to build before deciding how to build it.

When grown-ups say it

Design Thinking
A problem-solving method that meets readers directly instead of guessing. It loops through five stages, again and again.
Empathize
The first stage — meeting the reader in person. This is where desk-guessing stops.
Define
The stage of pinning down what's really confusing. It narrows 'a good book' down to one concrete problem.
Ideate
The stage of throwing out as many ideas as possible. Good or bad gets sorted out later.
Prototype
A cheap, quickly made sample page. It's for checking, not for shipping.
Requirements Engineering
A more systematic process of asking before writing. It digs deeper into the empathize/define stages. → the one who asks before writing
A/B Test
A test that measures, with numbers, which of two options works better. It goes deeper into the test stage with statistics. → which cover really sells better
Usability Test
Having people actually try the prototype and watching them. Often paired with the test stage. → the test where a reader just tries the book cold
