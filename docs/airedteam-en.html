What is an AI Red Team?

An AI red team, told as a story about a hygiene inspector's surprise visit — deliberately attacking this kitchen to find its weaknesses.

Normally it serves guests just fine.But the inspector shows up without warning — to find the weaknesses before a real attacker does.

It can be fooled a different way each time.Role-play, code, asking sideways — one test alone can't catch them all.

An AI red team is a dedicated team that deliberately writes strange orders to test the kitchen first.They write smuggled notes just like a real attacker would, and record it when the chef falls for one.

A general red team and an AI red team target different things.The security world's red team targets fences, locks, fake phone calls. An AI red team targets the words written in the order itself.

In one breath

An AI red team is a dedicated team that deliberately writes strange orders to find this kitchen's weaknesses first, before it opens to real guests.

An AI red team is a team of people who deliberately and creatively try LLM-specific techniques — jailbreak attempts, prompt attacks, role-play — against an AI model to find its weaknesses. Unlike a general security red team that targets networks or phishing, an AI red team treats the words fed into the model itself as the attack surface.
