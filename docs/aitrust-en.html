What is AI Trustworthiness?

AI trustworthiness, told as a story about a certification board that accuracy alone can't pass — one that also checks fairness, robustness, explainability, and accountability.

They want the certification mark.One number alone can't say whether it's really trustworthy.

Bias and weak spots hide behind the accuracy number.It might be biased toward one crop, or easily fooled by a tiny scratch.

AI trustworthiness is a certification earned only by meeting all five criteria.High accuracy alone doesn't earn this grade.

Check fairnessdoes it score evenly across crops?
Check robustnessdeliberately test with tampering
Check explainabilitycan it say why it scored this way?
Check accountabilitywho answers when something goes wrong?

The five criteria are checked one by one.If even one falls short, the whole certification is put on hold.

Getting certified once isn't the end.AI trustworthiness is an ongoing, re-checked state.

In one breath

AI trustworthiness is a certification that needs accuracy plus fairness, robustness, explainability, and accountability all at once — and must be re-checked continuously.

Trustworthy AI is a concept shared across frameworks like the OECD principles and the EU AI Act: beyond raw performance (accuracy), it requires fairness (bias mitigation), robustness (resilience to adversarial attacks and out-of-distribution input), explainability (XAI), and accountability (governance and human oversight). It is a balance across several axes rather than a single metric, and needs re-evaluation whenever the data or use context changes.
