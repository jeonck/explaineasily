What is Project Conflict Management?

A story about the author and editor clashing over a deadline — and choosing the right way to work it out.

The author and editor clash over the deadline.When two people want different things, friction is bound to happen.

Left unaddressed, it only grows.A small disagreement piles up into a bigger conflict.

Conflict management = choosing the right resolution style for the moment.The point isn't erasing conflict — it's knowing how to handle it.

Collaboratesolve it together, both satisfied
Compromiseeach side gives a little
Smooth / Accommodatewhen it's not urgent, one side yields
Forcewhen urgent, decide and push through

In practice there are five styles.Withdrawing is an option too, but overusing it just lets conflict pile up.

Conflict management doesn't make conflict disappear.It's not the skill of erasing it — it's the skill of handling it.

In one breath

Project conflict management = handling disagreements among stakeholders using the style that fits the moment (collaborate, compromise, accommodate, force, withdraw) to reach a better outcome.

PMBOK groups conflict-resolution techniques into five: Collaborate/Problem Solve, Compromise, Smooth/Accommodate, Force/Direct, and Withdraw/Avoid. None is always correct — the right one depends on the conflict's urgency and importance.

When grown-ups say it

Conflict Management
The skill of handling disagreement. Not erasing conflict — handling it.
Collaborate
Solving it together so both sides win. Takes the most time, but gives the best result.
Compromise
Each side gives a little. Quick, but not fully satisfying for either side.
Accommodate
One side yields when it isn't urgent. Good for preserving the relationship.
Force
Pushing through using authority. Fast when urgent, but can hurt the relationship.
Withdraw
Postponing it for now. Overused, it just lets conflict pile up.
Stakeholder Management
Identifying the people involved before conflict even starts. A step of preparation that comes before conflict management.
IS Audit/PMO
Also helps coordinate conflicts across several books. Sometimes helps mediate at a wider scope. → the last checker before publication
