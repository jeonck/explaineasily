What is an AI Data Center?

AI data centers, told as a story about a building annex constructed just for power-hungry GPU equipment.

An AI tenant has brought in equipment that draws enormous power.Nothing like ordinary office equipment.

Ordinary office power and AC can't handle it.The breaker trips, and the AC can't keep up.

An AI data center is an annex built just for the GPUs.A separate power line and cooling pipe, built just for the GPU racks.

Power density is much higherfar more power packed per rack
Cooling is far more intenseeven liquid cooling gets used
Runs an ultra-fast dedicated networkGPUs need to talk to each other fast
Built only where power is abundantit can't go just anywhere

The AI-only annex goes further on all four measures.That's why it can't be built just anywhere.

Construction cost and securing enough power are both genuinely hard.Power supply itself can become the bottleneck.

In one breath

An AI data center is a dedicated annex with power, cooling, and networking all designed separately, just for power-hungry equipment like GPUs.

GPUs and accelerators used for AI training and inference draw far more power and generate far more heat than ordinary servers, requiring data centers purpose-built for high-density power delivery, liquid cooling, and low-latency high-speed interconnects between GPUs. Construction cost and securing enough power have become the new bottlenecks.

When grown-ups say it

AI data center
An annex built just for the GPUs. Power, cooling, and networking are all designed separately.
GPU / accelerator
The specialized equipment that draws enormous power. Speeds up AI training and inference.
Power density
How much power is packed into one rack. Much higher in an AI-only annex than in an ordinary building.
Liquid cooling
A cooling method stronger than just AC air. Needed because the heat output is just too much.
Interconnect
The dedicated wiring GPUs use to talk to each other fast. Has a big effect on training speed.
VM / Container
The two ways to divide space in an ordinary office. GPU equipment is sometimes divided this way too. → the private office and the partitioned desk
Scale-up / Scale-out
Making one office bigger, or renting more of them. AI annexes lean toward scale-up (beefier equipment). → bigger office or more offices
Cloud service model
Bare shell / interior done / move-in ready. GPU infrastructure can be rented as IaaS too. → pick one to rent
