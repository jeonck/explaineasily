What are QA and QC?

Watching that the editing process is followed, and reading the finished manuscript to mark it up, are two different jobs.

A reader found a typo — after everything was already printed.It's already in the reader's hands, so it's hard to undo.

Found late, it's both slow and expensive to fix.Fixing it after printing means reprinting everything.

QA checks how it's made; QC checks what came out of it.Both are needed to catch typos before they reach readers.

QA = process checkwatches the proofing procedure itself
QA = prevention-focusedaims to cause fewer errors up front
QC = product inspectionreads the finished manuscript directly
QC = find and mark flawsmarks each one with a red pen

QA and QC differ in scope and timing.QA looks broad and early; QC looks narrow and late.

Both are needed, but they play different roles.QC is the net that catches flaws; QA is the care that prevents them in the first place.

In one breath

QA checks whether the process followed the standard; QC inspects the finished product itself to find defects.

QA (Quality Assurance) checks adherence to process standards to prevent defects from happening; QC (Quality Control) directly inspects and tests the product to find defects that did happen. QA leans toward prevention, QC toward detection — they complement rather than replace each other.

When grown-ups say it

Quality Assurance
Checking whether the editing process was followed. Closer to prevention.
Quality Control
Directly inspecting the finished manuscript. Closer to detection.
Verification
Checking "did we build it right." Leans closer to QA.
Validation
Checking "did we build the right thing." Leans closer to QC and testing.
Defect
A typo left in the manuscript. What QC directly hunts for.
ISO 9001
An international standard for quality management systems. One external standard QA can follow.
IS Audit/PMO
Checking quality and schedule across many books at once. Looks at an even wider scope than QA. → the last checker before publication
CMMI
How mature an organization's quality process is. Consistent QA work is what raises this maturity.
