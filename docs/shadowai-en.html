What is Shadow AI?

Shadow AI, told as a story about a delivery box an employee quietly brings in through the back door, without the company knowing.

Tired of waiting for approval, someone brings something else in through the back.The official request is slow — the back door works right away.

The employee just wanted to finish faster — the manager can't see this box at all.It's not malice — it's just invisible, so nobody knew.

Shadow AI = an outside tool quietly brought in and used, without the company knowing.The front door checks layer by layer — nobody even knows about the back-door box.

The same task is handled completely differently at the front door vs. the back.Approval, checking, record-keeping, and access — all four differ.

You find out only after it breaks — a ban alone doesn't stop it.A faster front door shrinks shadow AI more than a ban does. AI governance is writing down what the sanctioned tools are — shadow AI is exactly what happens quietly outside that board.

In one breath

Shadow AI = an outside AI tool an employee quietly brings in through the back door, without the company knowing — skipping approval, review, and record-keeping alike.

Shadow AI refers to employees using AI tools the company hasn't approved or even knows about. Because it bypasses IT's formal review, guardrails, and logging, data leaks, supply-chain risk, or policy violations can go undetected for a long time. A ban notice alone rarely reduces it — the sanctioned tool has to be fast and easy enough to use instead.
