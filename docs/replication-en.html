In one breath

Replication = keeping the same log copied across several warehouses. The leader handles writes and the rest just copy, so the service survives even if one warehouse fails.

Copying data across multiple servers. There's a leader and followers; synchronous replication updates immediately, asynchronous replication catches up a bit later (consistency). When a follower takes over from a failed leader, that's called failover.

When grown-ups say it

Replication
Keeping the same log in several warehouses. The service keeps running even if one warehouse fails.
Leader/follower
The warehouse that writes, and the ones that just copy. There's one leader, but there can be many followers.
Replication lag
The short time it takes to copy over. During that gap a follower may answer with slightly old data.
Read replica
A follower dedicated to reads. It takes read load off the leader.
Sync / async replication
Whether copying happens instantly or a bit later. Instant is safer but slower.
Multi-region
Putting a follower in a whole different city. It survives even if one whole city has trouble.
Eventual consistency
Getting a slightly different answer while copying is still in progress. Given enough time, every copy agrees.
Failover
A follower stepping up to be leader when the leader fails. Deciding who becomes the new leader is a vote. → control rooms raising hands to decide
