What is AI Data-Privacy Compliance?

AI data-privacy compliance, told as a story about checking a grower list — that rode along with the crops — against a legal checklist, and keeping the paperwork.

The grower hands over the whole list to the sorter operator.We thought only the crops came — but people's info came along too.

The inspector warns that skipping the law breaks the rules.Unlike crops, people's info can only be used as far as they agreed.

In one breath

AI data-privacy compliance = before feeding people's info into the sorter, checking purpose, consent, retention, and cross-border transfer against a legal checklist and keeping the paperwork.

Under laws like Korea's PIPA or GDPR, using personal data for AI training or inference requires stating the collection purpose, obtaining data-subject consent, setting a retention period, meeting extra requirements for cross-border transfer (e.g. standard contractual clauses), and running a Privacy Impact Assessment. Technical masking and pseudonymization are one *means* of meeting these requirements — they don't replace compliance itself.

When grown-ups say it

PIPA · GDPR
The law that governs how the list may travel. Sets the conditions for collecting, using, and sharing personal data.
Consent
The grower's own signature of permission. If the purpose changes, consent must be obtained again.
Retention period
How long the list stays in the cabinet. Once it expires, dispose of it or get re-approval.
Cross-border transfer
The list traveling to a warehouse in another country. Extra requirements like standard contractual clauses apply.
Privacy Impact Assessment
A risk check done before a big job starts. Often mandatory before high-risk processing begins.
Pseudonymized data
The list with names swapped for tags. The result of technical masking. → the secret beans we gave the parrot
AI Governance
The operating rules board for the whole farm. Privacy compliance is one pillar of governance. → the rules board on the wall
AI Trustworthiness
The whole certification board — fairness, safety, transparency too. Privacy is one piece that makes up trustworthiness. → the certification board
