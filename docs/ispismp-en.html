What is ISP/ISMP?

ISP (information strategy planning) and ISMP (information system master plan), told as a story about mapping out what a publishing house will put out ahead of time.

Books get started on whatever's at hand.Nobody knows what should come out first.

Chasing only bestsellers loses the house's overall direction.Chasing trends alone loses the house's own identity.

ISP/ISMP draws the big picture of what to publish, and in what order.It comes long before audit — it's the plan itself.

Draw the big picture firstyears of publishing direction ahead
Study the readershipwho we're selling to
Watch rival houseswhat others are publishing
Set the orderlining up what comes first

The roadmap is drawn by looking at these four things.ISP covers the whole house; ISMP breaks that roadmap into a finer execution plan.

The roadmap doesn't write the book for you.It's the map, not the manuscript itself.

In one breath

ISP/ISMP = a big-picture roadmap drawn years ahead, deciding what to publish and in what order.

ISP (Information Strategy Planning) sets an organization's information direction and priorities in line with its business strategy, while ISMP (Information System Master Plan) breaks that strategy down into a concrete system-build plan. Both are drawn at the whole-organization level, before any individual project begins.

When grown-ups say it

ISP
Setting the direction for the whole house. Lines up IT priorities with business strategy.
ISMP
The roadmap broken into a finer execution plan. Comes right after ISP.
IS Audit/PMO
The last checker before publication. Comes after ISP/ISMP, once the project is actually running. → the last checker before publication
Requirements Engineering
Taking down one book's own outline precisely. Happens for a single book, after the roadmap is set.
BPR
Overhauling the publishing process itself. Often discussed alongside ISP at the organizational level.
Architecture Principles
The shared style guide every book must follow. A rule to follow while carrying out the roadmap.
Resource Allocation
Deciding which writer goes on which book. Gets worked out concretely at the ISMP stage.
Feasibility Study
Checking in advance whether the book can really be made. Done before a book even makes it onto the roadmap.
