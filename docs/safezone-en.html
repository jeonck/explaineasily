What is a Personal-Data Safe Zone?

A personal-data safe zone, told as a story about a special reading room where several castles' ledgers can be viewed together, but never carried out.

Neither castle's ledger may leave its own walls.Combining them would give a far better answer — but each stays locked inside its own castle.

But sending it over in the open is dangerous.So everyone stays locked inside their own castle, and the better analysis never happens.

A safe zone is a reading room where ledgers are gathered in one place, viewed only inside, with only the result ever carried out.The ledgers themselves never leave the room at all.

Everyone who enters is loggedaccess control and a log

Using a safe zone follows a set process.Apply, get approved, analyze inside the room, then undergo one more check before anything leaves.

Approval can take a long time, and not everything is allowed out.If the result could still be re-identified, the export is refused outright.

In one breath

A personal-data safe zone = a special reading room where data from several organizations is gathered in one place, analyzed only inside, with only approved results ever exported.

A data safe zone gathers pseudonymized and linked data from multiple organizations into a physically and logically controlled space for analysis, while blocking the raw data from ever leaving and allowing only the analysis results out, after a re-identification risk review. A data linkage agency screens applications and performs the linkage, and an export review committee checks the results for re-identification risk before anything is released. The goal is to let organizations use combined data safely while keeping leak risk to a minimum.

When grown-ups say it

Personal-data safe zone
The special reading room. A controlled space where data from several organizations is analyzed, but the raw data never leaves.
Data linkage agency
The dedicated agency that combines the ledgers. Screens applications and professionally performs the linkage between pseudonymized datasets.
Export review
The check on the way out. An export review committee checks the result poses no re-identification risk.
Re-identification risk assessment
Measuring whether the name can come back. Even pseudonymized data can grow risky again once combined.
Pseudonymized data linkage
Stitching number tags together. Links pseudonymized records across organizations, enabling richer analysis.
Access control and logging
A record of who comes and goes. The same principle as the guest-info storeroom rulebook.
Safe data utilization
Using data without letting it out. An approach that extracts value from data without ever taking it out of its room.
Privacy-enhancing technologies
The toolbox. PET tools are often used inside the safe zone too, to cut risk further. → the toolbox with many different ways to protect a guest
